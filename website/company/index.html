About us

Software company behind Pocket Ledger

We build practical accounting tools for real businesses: party ledgers, banks, staff, tax, vouchers, attachments, and reports — on Windows desktop, Android, and the web. Our product philosophy is local-first: your books stay on hardware you control, with sharing you turn on when it suits your team.

Pocket Ledger is our product name and brand (pocket-ledger.com). Day-to-day company books inside the app are separate — those are your business companies, not ours.
